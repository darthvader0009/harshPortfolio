About me

I am an Adobe Certified Expert AEM Developer with over 3 years of hands-on experience in AEM 6.5 development. My expertise includes working with AEM architecture, Sightly (HTL), JSP, Sling Models, Servlets (including OSGI), Dispatcher configurations, and front-end technologies like JavaScript, jQuery, HTML, and CSS.

I have a strong background in CI/CD pipelines and Azure DevOps, and I have consistently worked in Agile environments following SCRUM methodologies. I have successfully integrated AEM with Single Page Applications (SPA) frameworks such as Angular and React.

Additionally, I have been trained in the MEAN stack and possess working knowledge of headless CMS and AEM as a Cloud Service (AEMaaCS). My experience extends to AEM Multi-Site Management (MSM), where I have created and maintained live copies, language copies, and blueprints.
